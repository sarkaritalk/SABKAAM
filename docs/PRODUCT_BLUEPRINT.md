# SABKAAM — Product Blueprint

## 1. Vision

Build an accessible platform where people can find different kinds of work and employers can reach local candidates. Begin with Bihar and Jharkhand, then expand based on actual usage, safety, and operational capacity.

**Brand line:** हर काम, हर इंसान के लिए।

## 2. Initial launch region

- Bihar — all 38 districts.
- Jharkhand — all 24 districts.
- Initial coverage target — 62 districts.

Coverage is a product target, not a claim that listings or employer support already exist in every district.

## 3. Audiences

### Job seekers
People seeking full-time, part-time, daily-wage, seasonal, local, remote, or freelance work. The experience should work for first-time job seekers and people without a formal CV.

### Employers
Local shops, hotels, clinics, factories, offices, contractors, service providers, and larger organizations that need to find workers.

### Platform administrators
A moderation and support team responsible for reviewing reports, handling suspicious listings, and maintaining platform quality.

## 4. Opportunity categories

- Shops and retail: kirana, clothing, cosmetics, and other stores.
- Hotels, restaurants, catering, and hospitality.
- Hospitals, clinics, and care-related support roles.
- Factories, warehouses, and production.
- Companies, banks, and offices.
- Drivers, delivery, and logistics.
- Domestic and household work.
- Technical trades and repair services.
- Agriculture and related seasonal work.
- Education and training.
- Construction and skilled/unskilled site work.
- Security and facility support.
- Online work, remote work, and freelancing.
- Government recruitment information and official application links.

Work formats include full-time, part-time, daily wage, seasonal, work from home, and freelance.

## 5. Job seeker capabilities

- Select state and district, then narrow to town, market, or nearby area.
- Search by category and work format.
- Hindi-first search, with voice search as a planned accessibility feature.
- Browse visual category tiles.
- Read key job details: pay range, hours, location, requirements, contact method, and whether food or accommodation is offered.
- Apply with a simple profile; resume upload should be optional where possible.
- Contact an employer directly when the employer enables that option.
- Track applications, alerts, and interview reminders.

## 6. Employer capabilities

- Create and maintain an employer profile.
- Add job title, category, location, pay, hours, requirements, and benefits.
- Manage active, paused, and closed listings.
- Review applications and contact candidates.
- Access optional promoted listings or subscription features after pricing validation.

## 7. Trust, safety, and moderation

- Establish employer verification levels and explain what each level means.
- Provide a visible way to report suspicious jobs, employers, or requests for money.
- Review reports and remove or pause unsafe listings when appropriate.
- Warn users never to pay a recruiter or employer merely to secure a job.
- Avoid exposing candidate phone numbers or personal details without a clear user action and consent.
- Keep an audit trail for moderation decisions.
- Government recruitment pages must point to official sources and clearly state that SABKAAM is an information platform, not a government agency.

Verification should be described accurately; no badge should imply a guarantee of employment or absolute safety.

## 8. Business model hypotheses

Potential employer-side revenue streams:
- One-time job posting.
- Monthly employer plans.
- Featured or promoted listings.
- Bulk hiring tools and candidate management.

Previously discussed indicative prices (₹99–199 per job; ₹299, ₹699, and ₹999 monthly tiers) are hypotheses only. They are not final prices and must be tested with real employers before launch.

Job seekers should be able to discover basic opportunities without being forced into a paid plan. Any paid feature must be clearly described before purchase.

## 9. Admin and operations

The platform will need:
- Employer and listing review.
- User reports and dispute handling.
- Category and location management.
- Listing expiry and duplicate detection.
- Basic analytics for searches, applications, and employer activity.
- Support contact and moderation history.

## 10. Phased delivery

### Phase 1 — Foundation
Finalize product requirements, user journeys, visual system, technology choices, and repository conventions.

### Phase 2 — Usable MVP
Build responsive job discovery, location/category filters, job details, candidate profiles, employer posting, and basic application/contact flows.

### Phase 3 — Trust and operations
Add moderation tools, reporting, employer verification workflow, listing expiry, and operational dashboards.

### Phase 4 — Growth features
Evaluate voice search, local-language support, notifications, audio/video job explanations, employer subscriptions, and promoted listings.

### Phase 5 — Expansion
Expand beyond Bihar and Jharkhand only after the initial region has reliable supply, user support, and safety operations.

## 11. Success measures

Measure real outcomes rather than downloads alone:
- Active and complete job listings.
- Employers who receive relevant applicants.
- Candidate applications and successful employer contacts.
- Time to first useful result.
- Repeat usage by candidates and employers.
- Report resolution time and suspicious-listing rate.
- Employer retention and willingness to pay.

## 12. Open decisions before production coding

- Final frontend/backend stack and hosting.
- Authentication and phone verification approach.
- Database and file storage.
- Location data source and radius-search implementation.
- Messaging and notification provider.
- Payment provider and applicable compliance.
- Privacy policy, terms, data retention, and consent flows.
- Moderation staffing and employer verification rules.

These decisions should be made deliberately before production credentials, personal data, or payment information are introduced.
